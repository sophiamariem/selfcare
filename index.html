                  Combinations

                  • One strong thing a day. Pads, Reedle, the wand and Medicube Booster are the strong ones: never two on the same day
                  • Nothing pushed deeper. Booster and Reedle only with hydrating products, never over Differin, azelaic, vitamin C, niacinamide 10%, TXA or acids

                  • Calm, closed skin only. No device or active on skin that is broken, peeling, sunburnt, swollen or freshly picked
                  • Around any treatment, everything stops with the actives and comes back in the order on the Treatments tab

                  General rules for each treatment, so the routine around it is already decided. The Procedure tab has the dated plan for the one coming up.

                  Rules that apply to all of them

                  • One procedure at a time, 2+ weeks apart.
                  • No actives on skin that is open, peeling, swollen or pink.
                  • Strict SPF for 4 weeks after anything that resurfaces or heats.
                  • No picking, steam, sauna or hard workouts for 48 hours.
                  • Devices stop with the actives and come back in stages: LED once the skin has closed (earlier only if the clinic uses it), the jade roller when Differin restarts, gua sha, Medicube MC and Derma Shot when Differin is nightly, and Booster and the wand last, with the full routine. The Procedure tab puts dates on it.
                  • Injectables (Botox, filler): no wand, Medicube, gua sha, roller or face pressure on the area for 2 weeks; LED is fine from the next day.
                  • Clinic instructions beat this page.

                  What goes on healing skin, in order of how early it's allowed

                  2. Plain hydrator + Illiyoon or Aestura
                  3. Rejuran mask or cream, sheet masks
                  4. Eye creams
                  5. Differin
                  6. Azelaic acid, brightening serums, vitamin C
                  7. Boosters (Vollufiline, NMN, Alpha-H)
                  8. Pads, spot treatments, Reedle Shot

                  Which treatment for which problem

                  • Red marks (PIE): vascular laser. Azelaic, niacinamide/TXA, SPF are the slow backup.
                  • Pitted / textured scars: microneedling, then fractional laser if it plateaus.
                  • Eye line: Botox. The retinal eye cream is already doing all a topical can.
                  • New pimples: Pair and pin patches early, then hands off.
