How to use each one

Tooth powder

Once a day

  • Wet your brush
  • Tap a little powder into the cap or your palm and dip the brush there, not into the jar
  • Brush gently for 2 min, spit, rinse
  • No fluoride on the label? Use your normal fluoride toothpaste at the other brushing

Whitening strips

3–4 nights a week

  • Apply to dry teeth, gel side on teeth
  • Press into gaps, fold extra behind teeth
  • Leave on for the time on the box (usually ~30 min)
  • Don't sleep in them unless the box says so

Whitening pen

2–3 nights a week, on non-strip nights

  • Twist until gel shows on the brush tip
  • Thin layer over the whole front of your smile teeth (top and bottom front 6–8)
  • Keep lips off for about 1 min
  • No food or drink for 30–60 min

Mint breath spray

Anytime

  • 1–2 sprays on the tongue
  • No waiting rules
  • Freshens breath only, it doesn't whiten

Golden milk

It won't stain your teeth permanently. Turmeric leaves a surface stain that brushing and whitening remove. Have it before your routine, never after. If it's sweetened, brushing afterward also protects against cavities.

Food and habits

Fine anytime

  • Water
  • Milk, plain yogurt, white cheese
  • Rice, pasta, chicken, white fish
  • Bananas, apples (peeled), cauliflower
  • Coffee before whitening, with a straw if iced, rinse after

Avoid right after whitening

  • Coffee, black tea, red wine, cola
  • Golden milk, turmeric, curry
  • Berries, beetroot, tomato sauce
  • Soy sauce, balsamic vinegar
  • Smoking or vaping

Take a break if…

  • Teeth feel zingy or achy: skip 2–3 nights and use a sensitivity toothpaste (potassium nitrate)
  • Gums turn white or sore: trim strips and wipe excess gel
  • You have cavities, gum problems, fresh dental work, or are pregnant or breastfeeding: check with a dentist first

Fillings, crowns and veneers don't whiten. Always follow the times printed on your own boxes if they differ from this sheet.
